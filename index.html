How the programme works

Four films, no two alike, go up on the board at midnight and stay for exactly 24 hours. Until the house has heard from you both, every title comes only from the list you keep in Google Keep — nothing else gets in.

After the film

Log what you watched, how it landed, and a line or two. The house keeps each of your tastes on file, separately and together. Once it knows you, it starts suggesting where to go next — Iran, Turkey, Brazil, Europe and beyond.
